# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publication of private information, and encouragement of unauthorized scanning are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools.
